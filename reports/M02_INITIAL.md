## Diagnosis and implementation

Inspected `MainActivity.java`, `FrameTelemetry.java`, native input/filter, `stroke/spline.{cpp,hpp}`, `native_bridge.cpp`, replay/smoke and tests listed in [source map](../docs/CODE_MAP.md). The baseline exact-fit reserve behavior is demonstrated below. Source inspection independently confirmed whole-curve snapshot/evaluation, bounds scans and raw copying under the pen-up mutex, three subdivision policies, fixed radius and deliberate equal-time spline omission. These observations alone did not establish visible glitches or tablet latency. The baseline had a bounded fitting window already; the expensive growth was in **retained** vectors. Full bridge timing on the archived baseline was run separately with the later benchmark harness.

* Input: retain original time and sequence, propagate spatial displacement but do not estimate a derivative or advance the temporal filter at zero elapsed time. Reject regressions. Time-only queries select the last tied sample; geometry uses knot order. Candidate filtering, vector reserves and spline append precede nonthrowing publication; failed samples may grow capacity but do not commit a point. Max-sample limit remains one million per stroke, **not** a practical memory budget.
* Geometry: for a cubic, the maximum of the endpoint second-derivative norms bounds `|P''|`. Use `ceil(sqrt(M/(8*tolerance)))` equal parameter intervals, capped at 64. Target **0.20 capture pixels before rounding and cap**; not a bound at arbitrary later zoom. One advancing stable segment plus at most one provisional segment per new knot. Geometry tests densely evaluate loops/reversals against chords. Pressure 0..1 maps to radius 0.665..2.85 capture pixels; the shader evaluates a swept variable-radius disc. A stationary peak deposits local ink, not a taper propagated backward along the incoming segment. Latest raw tip is drawn without waiting for another sample.
* Completion: immutable ordered raw samples, filter parameters and scale are authoritative in memory; meshes are derived. No storage schema. Reserve space during ingestion; append only the cached provisional part and terminal geometry, move histories/meshes, publish immutable shared owners in completion order, release live scratch and log outside the mutex. Bounds cover new geometry (obsolete tail bounds can overinclude); culling uses shader-quantized viewport. GL handles remain owned by the GL thread.

## Verification, review and limits

No owner handwriting recording was available. Host outputs: `captures/m02-final.txt`, `captures/m02-base-bridge.txt` (local ignored evidence).
* Separate real GLES2 shader link and RGBA8 pbuffer readback: **Mesa 26.0.3 llvmpipe**, not mock or hardware. Dots at both pressure limits, pressure dwell, monotonic taper, completed overlap order.
