**Decisive coverage counterexample.** Center a radius **0.15 px** dot at a pixel center (pressure 1, base radius 1, zoom 0.1). With 1 then 16 coincident alternating-color dots, compare production output to area integration: source-over the ordered colors *inside* the coincident discs, then weight by `π × 0.15²` over background. This is a physical box-filter reference, **not** a v1 reopen oracle. One dot: actual RGB **46/12/19**, area reference **10.4/13.8/22.6**. Sixteen: actual **110/3/131**, reference **13.2/13.0/31.6**. The current smoothstep AA footprint is not an area certificate at this scale. Neither omission nor treating individual per-stroke pixel averages as independent subpixel coverage is generally correct for coincident marks. This is not evidence of a new M03 regression. Paging and instancing alone fix neither the discrepancy nor the 2-draws-per-ID slope.
